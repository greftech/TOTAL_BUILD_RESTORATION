## Known cosmetic items, deliberately not fixed

- The Sheet Script's file header still calls itself a test bundle and says to
  update the sheet ID. Stale wording over a correct value; left alone at your
  request.
- Column A's green/red flag is painted by hand, not driven by the YES/NO value.
  Because formatting now inherits from the row above, a NO lead landing under a
  YES row inherits green. Converting column A to conditional formatting fixes
  that permanently. See the design spec.
- Dropdowns on archive rows are inconsistent: a job moved into Completed inherits
  them, one appended to JNS does not.
